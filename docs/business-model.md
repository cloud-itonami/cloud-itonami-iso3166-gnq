# Business model — GNQ

Independent market-entry compliance for Equatorial Guinea.

- No verifiable national public-procurement law, regulator or
  e-tendering portal could be independently confirmed this iteration
  (OHADA has no procurement uniform act; CEMAC's public-finance-
  management directives do not cover procurement either -- both
  independently confirmed via `ohada.org`/`cemac.int`). This vertical's
  market-entry mechanism is therefore company/business registration, not
  procurement-bid filing (see `src/marketentry/facts.cljc` for the full
  list of sources checked, including several dead/parked/blocked
  domains, disclosed honestly rather than glossed over).
- OHADA member state -- company law is the Acte uniforme relatif au
  droit des sociétés commerciales et du groupement d'intérêt économique
  (AUSCGIE, adopted 30 January 2014, in force 5 May 2014), directly
  applicable without domestic transposition. Registration itself happens
  at the Commercial Registry (Registro de la Propiedad y Mercantil) --
  World Bank Doing Business 2020's own text ties this registration step
  to OHADA AUSCGIE Art. 261 (a notice of incorporation must be published
  in a legal journal within 15 days of registration).
- Additional registration touchpoints (World Bank Doing Business 2020's
  own text): Ministry of Commerce, Department of Business and Private
  Investment; Tax Authorities / Public Treasury (tax identification
  number); Ministry of Labor (inspection book, labor calendar, Fondo de
  Protección del Trabajador contribution); an alternate "One-Stop-Shop"
  ("VUE") for notarization, tax registration and the Ministry of Finance
  solvency certificate.
- World Bank Doing Business 2020's own documented paid-in minimum-
  capital requirement of XAF 1,000,000 for a Limited Liability Company
  (SARL) in Malabo -- this vertical's flagship governor check
  independently validates an engagement's own declared paid-in capital
  against this floor before any filing/submit proposal can commit.

## Trust Controls

Any actual Commercial Registry (Registro de la Propiedad y Mercantil) /
Ministry of Commerce filing submission requires Market-Entry Compliance
Governor clearance and always escalates to human sign-off. A false or
fabricated regulatory-requirement claim is a HARD hold that no human
approval can override.
