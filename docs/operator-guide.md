# Operator guide — GNQ

Portal: no verified public-procurement portal for Equatorial Guinea --
this actor's market-entry mechanism is company/business registration.
Entity registration: Commercial Registry (Registro de la Propiedad y
Mercantil, tied by World Bank Doing Business 2020 to OHADA AUSCGIE Art.
261) plus Ministry of Commerce, Department of Business and Private
Investment enrollment. Tax: tax identification number via Tax
Authorities / Public Treasury (or the "One-Stop-Shop"/"VUE"
alternative). Engagements also clear the World Bank Doing Business 2020
XAF 1,000,000 paid-in minimum-capital floor gate (Commercial Registry,
SARL) before a filing can proceed. Human-gated filing only.
